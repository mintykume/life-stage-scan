ok here's everything. I'm 29, product designer at a fintech in Warsaw for 4 years. Good at it, promoted twice, my parents are proud. Honestly though the best hours of my week are Tuesday nights when I teach a free ceramics class at the community center, I lose track of time completely, and I've been 'thinking about' doing something with ceramics for like three years. Growing up I admired my aunt who ran a bakery and my art teacher who never seemed to care what anyone thought. Favorite film is one where a burned-out chef quits a fancy restaurant and starts a food truck. I spend money without guilt on clay, kiln time and train tickets. I'm a bit envious of a friend who runs a small studio, not the money, the fact that her days are hers. When I got conflicting advice about switching teams last year I basically did what my manager suggested. I keep complaining that design reviews are all about metrics and nobody talks about how things feel. Rent is fine and I have some savings. You have enough now, do the full life scan: where am I and what should I do?
